House Rules

  1. Two teams compete.
  2. Each team gets up to 60 seconds per turn.
  3. Teams discuss the clue and choose a location on the world map.
  4. The team’s nominated spokesperson or clicker places the official pin.
  5. Once a guess is locked in, it cannot change unless the facilitator resets that team’s turn.
  6. The second team never sees the first team’s pin before locking in its own guess.
  7. After both teams guess, the answer is revealed.
  8. The team with the highest total score wins.
  9. A tie after the final round triggers an Expert Sudden Death question.

Scoring

  • 3 Your pin is inside the correct country’s boundary.
  • 2 Incorrect, but the closer of the two guesses.
  • 1 Incorrect and further away.
  • 0 No guess locked in before time ran out.
  • Both teams in the correct country: both score 3.
  • Both incorrect at the same rounded distance: both score 2.
  • If one team finds the correct country, the other team’s incorrect guess scores 1.
  • If only one team submits, it is scored normally (a lone incorrect guess counts as the closer guess) and the other team scores 0.
  • If neither team submits, both score 0 and the facilitator can replay or continue.
  • Solo or single-team play: 3 for the correct country, 2 if the pin is within 1,000 km of the answer, 1 if further, 0 for no pin. Your final score is shown out of the maximum possible.
  • If time runs out while a pin is placed but not locked, that pin is locked automatically.
  • Distances are measured to the answer marker. A pin in the sea within 50 km of the answer, or within 25 km of the correct coastline, counts as the correct country.

Online play

  • 1 to 6 teams. Every player joins on their own device with the room code. The facilitator can add or remove teams in the lobby and during the game.
  • Playing alone? Host the game, then use Play on this device too to open your own player screen. A single team uses solo scoring: 3 for the correct country, 2 within 1,000 km, 1 further away.
  • Each team chooses a team leader: a unanimous vote, a volunteer (unless a teammate objects), or “let fate decide”.
  • All teams guess at the same time during the 60 seconds. Teammates drop suggestion pins that only their own team can see; only the leader places and locks the official pin.
  • Scoring with more than two teams: 3 for the correct country; if no team is correct, the closest guess scores 2 (ties share it) and the others 1; if any team is correct, incorrect guesses score 1; no pin scores 0.
  • When the timer ends, any pin the leader has placed is locked automatically.
  • A tie for first place sends only the tied teams to Expert Sudden Death.

    About Where in the World?

    An original team game about where foods, traditions, objects, music, inventions, wildlife, games, buildings, crafts and natural wonders come from — and the people and cultures behind them.

    Our approach to culture

    Lived experience is treated as expertise. Questions were written to be respectful and factually supported, to note shared or disputed origins, and to avoid sacred or restricted material. Where an item belongs to several countries, all of them are accepted. If someone in the room knows more about a topic — invite them to share!

    About the map

    Country boundaries come from Natural Earth, Admin 0 – Countries, 1:50m scale, version 5.1.2 (public domain), drawn with the Leaflet library. This map is not politically neutral or perfectly authoritative: border and territory representations vary depending on legal and political perspective, and Natural Earth follows its own documented de facto policy. Some territories (for example Greenland, Puerto Rico and Western Sahara) appear as separate shapes. Coastlines are simplified, so small islands may look approximate.

    To be fair to islands and coastlines, a pin in the sea within 50 km of the answer marker, or within 25 km of the correct country’s coastline, counts as the correct country. Distances are great-circle (haversine) distances to the answer marker, rounded to the nearest kilometre.

    Keyboard

    • Tab through every control; Enter or Space activates buttons.
    • Answers are placed by clicking or tapping on the map (drag the pin to move it). On the focused map, arrow keys pan and + and − zoom.
    • Shortcuts (when not typing): L lock in, P pause/resume, M mute.
    • Optional soundboard shortcuts use Shift + number, so they never clash with map keys.

    Content review

    Questions have been reviewed internally against the sources listed with each answer. We recommend a review by people from the communities represented before high-profile use. Corrections are welcome — see README.md.
