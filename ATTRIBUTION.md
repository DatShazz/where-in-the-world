## Audio

All audio is original and synthesised at runtime with the Web Audio API in `game.js`. No third-party recordings, voices, songs, TV themes or branded sounds are used. Audio-clue impressions (steelpan, kora, valiha, talking drum, đàn bầu, candombe, coquí) are clearly labelled as synthesised impressions, not field recordings.
